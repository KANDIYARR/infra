About Kenmark Solutions

Delivering mission-critical infrastructure solutions and multivendor brand technical support across Africa and the Middle East.

Our Strategic Foundation

From Established Reliability to Advanced Infrastructure Excellence

With extensive experience in the agricultural commodities sector, our company has established a strong reputation for reliability, integrity, and customer-centric service. As markets and technologies have evolved, we have broadened our strategic focus to meet the increasing demand for mission-critical infrastructure solutions.

Today, we are proud to deliver specialized solutions for data centers, including precision cooling systems, UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) solutions, power infrastructure, installation, commissioning, preventive maintenance, and annual maintenance contracts (AMC).

Our team combines deep industry expertise with trusted technology partners to provide reliable, energy-efficient, and high-performance solutions that ensure maximum uptime for our clients’ critical operations.

Building on a strong business foundation and an unwavering commitment to quality, we continue to expand into advanced technology sectors while upholding the core values that have driven our long-standing success.
