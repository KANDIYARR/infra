Our Infrastructure Expertise

Comprehensive mission-critical infrastructure solutions designed to ensure reliability, efficiency, and continuous operation for data centers, telecom facilities, and critical environments.

UPS Systems & Critical Power Solutions

Kenmark specializes in the design, supply, installation, testing, and commissioning of Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) systems to protect critical loads from power interruptions and electrical disturbances. We ensure continuous and stable power availability for data centers, financial institutions, healthcare facilities, and mission-critical applications.

  • UPS system capacity assessment and load calculation
  • Power protection design and system selection
  • UPS installation, commissioning, and performance testing
  • Battery bank supply, replacement, and maintenance
  • Preventive maintenance and troubleshooting
  • Power quality analysis and optimization

Precision Cooling Systems

Maintaining the correct temperature and humidity conditions is essential for data center reliability. Kenmark provides advanced precision cooling solutions to maintain optimal environmental conditions for sensitive IT equipment, preventing overheating and extending equipment operational life.

  • CRAC / CCU precision air conditioning systems
  • Cooling capacity assessment and system selection
  • Server room cooling analysis and hot spot identification
  • Airflow management and cooling optimization
  • Installation, commissioning, and servicing
  • Preventive maintenance and energy efficiency improvement

Diesel Generator Systems

Kenmark delivers dependable backup power solutions to ensure business continuity during utility power failures. We help organizations maintain uninterrupted operations with reliable standby power solutions.

  • Generator capacity assessment and load calculation
  • Supply, installation, and commissioning support
  • Synchronization and integration with critical power systems
  • Preventive maintenance and performance testing
  • Fuel system and operational reliability checks
  • Automatic transfer switch (ATS) integration

Water Leak Detection Systems

Water leakage can create significant risks in data centers and critical facilities. Kenmark provides intelligent water leak detection solutions designed to identify and prevent potential damage to minimize downtime and protect infrastructure assets.

  • Leak detection cable and sensor installation
  • Monitoring system integration
  • Early warning alerts and alarm configuration
  • Protection for raised floors, cooling systems, and critical areas
  • Zone-based & addressable leak locating
  • Automated shut-off valve integration

Data Center Environmental Monitoring Systems

Kenmark provides advanced environmental monitoring solutions to maintain visibility and control over critical data center conditions. By providing real-time visibility, we help customers identify potential risks, improve efficiency, and maintain a highly reliable operating environment.

  • Temperature and humidity monitoring
  • Rack-level environmental monitoring
  • Power and energy monitoring
  • Alarm management and notification systems
  • Integration with BMS / DCIM platforms
  • Real-time telemetry & historical audit reporting
